# Email outage response procedure

Keywords: email, outage, outlook, mailbox, incident, messaging, down

When the email service status is 'outage':
1. Do not ask users to reinstall their mail client; the fault is central.
2. Escalate to the Messaging team as a high-priority incident if a whole team or function is affected.
3. Share the status page link and advise that urgent messages go through the team chat channel.
4. Link duplicate tickets to the main incident.
