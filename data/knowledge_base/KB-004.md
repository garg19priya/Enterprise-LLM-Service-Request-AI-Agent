# Change freeze and restart policy for finance systems

Keywords: payroll, erp, restart, reboot, change freeze, approval, finance

Payroll and Finance ERP may only be restarted under an approved change.
During the monthly payroll run (the 15th to the 20th) a change freeze applies: no restarts without sign-off from the Finance Systems service owner.
Any restart request must be recorded and approved by a human before it is carried out.
