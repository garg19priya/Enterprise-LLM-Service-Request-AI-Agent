# Resetting your VPN token

Keywords: vpn, token, remote access, authenticator, reset

If your VPN token is out of sync or lost:
1. Open the self-service portal and choose 'Reset VPN token'.
2. Confirm your identity with the code sent to your registered phone.
3. Re-enrol the authenticator app by scanning the new QR code.
4. Reconnect to the VPN. If the reset fails twice, contact the Network team. Never share token codes with anyone.
