# Category and urgency guide

Keywords: urgency, priority, category, triage, critical, high, medium, low

Categories: access (accounts, passwords, tokens, permissions); network (VPN connectivity, Wi-Fi); application (business systems such as email, CRM, payroll, ERP, HR portal, video conferencing); hardware (printers, laptops, peripherals); facilities (building systems, HVAC, server room environment); information (policy and general questions).
Urgency: critical = safety risk, or a business-critical service down for many teams; high = a service down for a whole team or function, or an environmental alarm above threshold; medium = one user blocked, or a degraded service with a workaround; low = how-to questions and information requests.
